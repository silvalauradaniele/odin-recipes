Cheesy Garlic Bread

Description

This irresistibly easy, cheesy garlic bread recipe is loaded with melted butter and Parmesan cheese and transforms French bread into a show-stopping treat.

Baked to golden perfection in less than twenty minutes, it offers a heavenly crunch on the outside while remaining incredibly soft, warm, and savory on the inside. The rich aroma of toasted garlic and fresh herbs makes it the ultimate comfort food—perfect as a quick snack, a crowd-pleasing appetizer, or the ultimate side dish to elevate your favorite soups and pasta nights.

Recipe Information

  • Prep Time:
    10 mins
  • Cook Time:
    15 mins
  • Total Time:
    25 mins
  • Servings:
    4

Ingredients

  • 1 (8 ounce) loaf French bread
  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • ⅓ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried parsley

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  2. Cut bread into 1-inch thick slices, without cutting all the way through the loaf. Combine butter, Parmesan cheese, garlic powder, and parsley in a small bowl; spread between bread slices and over top of loaf. Wrap loaf in aluminum foil, leaving top partially uncovered.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven until butter melts and bread toasts, 15 to 20 minutes.
